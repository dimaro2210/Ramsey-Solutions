## The Proven Plan That Works

You work too hard to live paycheck to paycheck. Whether you’re struggling with debt or building your savings, the [7 Baby Steps](https://www.ramseysolutions.com/dave-ramsey-7-baby-steps) will help you take control of your money for good.

![](https://cdn.ramseysolutions.net/media/homepage/2025/02-testing/digmktg-33066/baby-step-1.png)

**Baby Step 1**

### Save $1,000 for your starter emergency fund.

![](https://cdn.ramseysolutions.net/media/homepage/2025/02-testing/digmktg-33066/baby-step-2.png)

**Baby Step 2**

### Pay off all debt (except the house) using the debt snowball.

![](https://cdn.ramseysolutions.net/media/homepage/2025/02-testing/digmktg-33066/baby-step-3.png)

**Baby Step 3**

### Save 3–6 months of expenses in a fully funded emergency fund.

![](https://cdn.ramseysolutions.net/media/homepage/2025/02-testing/digmktg-33066/baby-step-4.png)

**Baby Step 4**

### Invest 15% of your household income in retirement.

![](https://cdn.ramseysolutions.net/media/homepage/2025/02-testing/digmktg-33066/baby-step-5.png)

**Baby Step 5**

### Save for your children's college fund.

![](https://cdn.ramseysolutions.net/media/homepage/2025/02-testing/digmktg-33066/baby-step-6.png)

**Baby Step 6**

### Pay off your home early.

![](https://cdn.ramseysolutions.net/media/homepage/2025/02-testing/digmktg-33066/baby-step-7.png)

**Baby Step 7**

### Build wealth and give.
